# PulsePoint

**A retention-focused fitness product concept for helping new users build a lasting workout habit.**

PulsePoint's product direction centers on a simple question: what would make someone come back to work out next week? This portfolio project translates the PRD into a plan for useful daily guidance, visible progress, and lightweight motivation, with success measured by continued activity rather than feature count.

> **Project status:** Planning. This repository currently contains product documentation; application code, screenshots, and a deployed demo are not available yet.

## The Problem

Many new users stop using PulsePoint within their first two weeks. The PRD points to declining motivation and an experience users perceive as outdated. This contributes to falling app-store ratings and organic downloads.

## Product Direction

Help users know what to do next, recognize the progress they have made, and stay in control of how the app reaches them.

## Proposed MVP

- **Daily recommendation:** One actionable suggestion, paired with a short explanation.
- **Progress insights:** Plain-language summaries grounded in recorded workout history.
- **Streaks and milestones:** Supportive recognition of consistency, with optional milestone sharing.
- **Notification preferences:** Simple controls that respect each user's choices.
- **Experience polish:** A more current presentation and less friction in onboarding, without changing workout logging.

The MVP intentionally excludes workout-logging changes, pricing or monetization work, leaderboards, and friend feeds.

## How Success Will Be Measured

- **North star:** Percentage of new users who log at least one workout in week three.
- **Supporting measure:** App-store rating.
- **Diagnostic measure:** Average session length per visit; this is not a standalone optimization target.

The PRD does not define numerical targets. Baselines and measurement rules should be agreed before implementation.
